About

The Utah AI Safety Hub is an independent, nonpartisan community for people in Utah who want advanced AI to be safe and good for everyone.

Why a local hub

Hubs in Buenos Aires, London, Singapore, Toronto, and New York started the same way: a few people, a reading group, a borrowed room. Over time they became communities that train researchers, run fellowships, and give people a place to work. As Li-Lian Ang writes in “We need more local AI safety hubs”, talent comes first. Funding and strategy follow.

Why Utah

Policy

A state that’s paying attention

In 2024 Utah created the Office of Artificial Intelligence Policy, the first of its kind in the country. Much of today’s AI policy is being written by states.

Research

Strong universities

The University of Utah, BYU, Utah Valley University, and Utah State train thousands of computing and policy students. Alignment research is already happening on campus.

Industry

Builders

Utah’s tech industry is full of engineers and founders who work with AI every day.

Who runs it

Emilio Barkett is an AI safety researcher who studies how large language models behave. He started the hub so people in Utah would have a local way into the field.

We’re looking for a few more organizers. If that’s you, sign up and tick “Volunteering.”

Questions

Do I need a technical background?

No. AI safety needs people from policy, law, communications, operations, education, and more. Our first reading group is built for newcomers.

Does it cost anything?

No. The reading group, co-working, and events are free.

Is this only for people in Salt Lake City?

No. The hub is for all of Utah, from Logan to St. George. Our first events are in Salt Lake City, and we’ll add groups where people sign up. Tell us where you are so we know where to grow.

Are you tied to a university, company, or party?

No. We’re independent and nonpartisan, and we hope to work with universities and community groups across the state.

How can I help?

Come to an event, bring a friend, or help organize. Tick “Volunteering” on the sign-up form and we’ll be in touch.
