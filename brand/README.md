# Utah AI Safety Hub logo kit

The logo is a tile: a solid square with five contour lines from the summit of Mount Timpanogos. The lines come from real elevation data (AWS Terrain Tiles, built mainly from USGS 3DEP data), the same source as the contours on the website.

## Colorways

| Name | Tile | Lines | Use on |
|---|---|---|---|
| `pine` | `#3d6b4f` | `#f7f8f4` | Light backgrounds. The default. |
| `paper` | `#f7f8f4` | `#3d6b4f` | Pine green backgrounds and photos. |
| `mint` | `#82bf96` | `#141813` | Dark backgrounds (`#141813`). |

## Files

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `svg/uash-tile-*.svg` | The tile on its own. Social avatars, app icons, stickers, slide corners. |
| `svg/uash-tile-*-small.svg` | Heavier lines for 16–48 px: favicons and small UI. |
| `svg/uash-logo-horizontal-*.svg` | Tile beside the name. Default for documents, slides, email signatures, and headers. |
| `svg/uash-logo-stacked-*.svg` | Tile above the name in spaced capitals. Posters, title slides, banners, merch. |
| `png/` | The same files as PNGs. Tiles at 512, 1024, and 2048 px; small tile at 16–192 px; lockups with transparent backgrounds. |
| `png/og-image.png` | 1200 × 630 link-preview image. |
| `../favicon.ico`, `../favicon.svg`, `../apple-touch-icon.png` | Browser and phone icons used by the website. |

Prefer the SVGs: they stay sharp at any size. The name is converted to outlines, so the files look the same on computers without the fonts. The fonts are Newsreader (the name) and Geist (spaced capitals), both free under the SIL Open Font License.

## Guidelines

- Use the `-small` tile at 48 px and below.
- Keep clear space around the logo of at least a quarter of the tile's height.
- Don't recolor the tile outside the three colorways, rotate or stretch it, or round its corners. (Phones round app icons on their own; that's fine.)
