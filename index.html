Utah AI Safety Hub

A community on the Wasatch Front for people who want to help advanced AI go well.

Join the mailing list See upcoming events

Why Utah

You don’t need to move to San Francisco or London to work on AI safety. Utah has strong universities, a fast-growing tech industry, and the first state office in the country dedicated to AI policy. What it doesn’t have yet is a place where people who care about AI safety can find each other and get started. We’re building one.

What we’re starting with

We’re starting small, with a few regular activities that anyone can join.

Reading group

A weekly discussion group working through an established AI safety curriculum, such as BlueDot Impact’s AGI Strategy course.

Co-working

A weekly day to work alongside others thinking about AI safety, whether you’re studying, researching, or building.

Events

Talks, Q&As, and socials in Salt Lake City and Utah County, starting with a kickoff event this fall.

Campus groups

Student reading groups at the University of Utah, BYU, UVU, and beyond.

Who it’s for

Anyone in Utah who takes the risks and opportunities of advanced AI seriously: students, engineers, researchers, policy professionals, and people from other fields who want to contribute. No prior background in AI safety is needed.
