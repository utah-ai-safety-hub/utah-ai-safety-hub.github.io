Get involved

There are a few ways to take part, whatever your background and however much time you have.

Activities

Weekly reading group

Work through a structured course with a small group, one session a week. We plan to start with BlueDot Impact’s AGI Strategy course.

Weekly co-working

Bring your laptop and work alongside others. We’ll start at a café or a borrowed room in Salt Lake City.

Events

Talks, Q&As, and socials. See what’s coming up.

Help organize

We’re building a small core team to run events, reading groups, and campus chapters.

For you

  • Students. Join a campus reading group at the U, BYU, UVU, or USU, or help start one at your school. Reading groups are a common first step into AI safety research and policy careers.
  • Engineers and researchers. Learn where technical AI safety research stands, meet others working on it, and find out about fellowships and programs that can help you contribute.
  • Policy professionals. Utah is one of the most active states on AI policy. Connect with others thinking about how AI should be governed at the state and national level.
  • Everyone else. AI safety needs people from many fields, including communications, operations, law, and education. Start with the reading group and see where it leads.
