A place for people who want AI to go well.

AI is getting more capable every year. Making it safe is one of the most important problems of our time, and it needs far more people working on it than it has.

Most of that work happens in a few cities. It doesn’t have to. Utah has strong universities, a growing tech industry, and the country’s first state office for AI policy. What it hasn’t had is a place for the people who care about this to find each other.

So we’re starting one, small: a reading group, a weekly co-working day, and events in Salt Lake City and Utah County.
