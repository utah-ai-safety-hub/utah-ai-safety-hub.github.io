Ways into the field

Programs run by other organizations that are open to people outside the main AI safety cities.

  • SPAR
    A part-time, remote research program that pairs aspiring AI safety researchers with mentors. Run by Kairos.
  • Pathfinder Fellowship
    Funding, mentorship, and support for students who organize AI safety groups at their university.
  • Generator Residency
    A paid three-month residency in Berkeley for generalists who want to build AI safety projects.
