Programs

We’re starting with a few simple, free activities. Everything is in person on the Wasatch Front. Sign up to hear when each one opens.

AGI Strategy reading group

A small discussion group working through BlueDot Impact’s AGI Strategy course, which covers how advanced AI could develop and what can be done to make it go well. Weekly, in person, no background needed.

Weekly co-working

A day each week to work alongside others thinking about AI safety, whether you’re studying, researching, or building. We’ll start at a café or a borrowed room in Salt Lake City.

Campus groups

Who it’s for

  • Students. Reading groups are a common first step into AI safety research and policy careers.
  • Engineers and researchers. Learn where technical AI safety work stands and meet others working on it.
  • Policy professionals. Utah is one of the most active states on AI policy. Connect with others thinking about how AI should be governed.
  • Everyone else. AI safety needs people from many fields, including communications, operations, law, and education.

Not sure where you fit?

Tell us a little about yourself when you sign up, and we’ll point you to the right place, including programs run by other organizations.
