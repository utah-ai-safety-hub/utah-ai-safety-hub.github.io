Programs

A few simple things to start, all free. The first ones are in Salt Lake City, and we’ll add more around the state as people join.

AGI Strategy reading group

A small group working through BlueDot Impact’s AGI Strategy course: how advanced AI could develop, and what people can do to make it go well. Weekly. No background needed.

Who it’s for

  • Students. A reading group is how many people in AI safety got started.
  • Engineers and researchers. See where technical safety work stands and meet the people doing it.
  • Policy people. Utah is one of the most active states on AI policy. Think it through with others who care how AI is governed.
  • Everyone else. This work needs communicators, operators, lawyers, teachers, and more.
