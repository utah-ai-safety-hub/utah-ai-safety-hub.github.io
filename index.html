Why AI safety

AI systems are getting more capable quickly. That brings real benefits, but also risks that could be hard to reverse if we get them wrong. Nobody knows exactly how it will go, and thoughtful people disagree about how serious the risks are. What’s clear is that the work of making AI safe still needs many more people, and that there’s room for newcomers to make a difference.

Why Utah

Utah has strong universities, a fast-growing tech industry, and the first state office in the country dedicated to AI policy. What it doesn’t have yet is a place where people who care about AI safety can find each other and get started. We’re building one.
