Fellowships

Getting into the field

Fellowships are how many people move into AI safety work, and most are open to people anywhere. These two guides, from people who’ve been through them, cover which ones to aim for and how to apply.

Which one

A Field Guide to AI Fellowships

By vivekstills, a recent Anthropic Fellow

Sorts fellowships into seven tracks, from research engineering and interpretability to policy and field-building. Includes a skills self-check and a directory of programs. Start by picking two or three tracks that appeal to you.

Read the guide →
How to apply

How to apply to AI safety fellowships (and beyond)

By Boyd Kane, MATS fellow

Make your skills easy for reviewers to check, write for the reviewer, start with lower-stakes programs like ARENA, and treat rejections as feedback. Especially useful if you’re far from the Bay Area or London.

Programs, from first step to full-time

Most people start with a free course, then apply to a research or policy fellowship. Adapted from the programs directory in “A Field Guide to AI Fellowships.” Dates and stipends change every cycle, so check each program’s own page.

Start here

Little or no background needed. Do one of these alongside everything else.

  • BlueDot Impact coursesOnline · cohorts year-round · free
  • SPARRemote, part-time · for people who can code a bit
  • ARENALondon · about 5 weeks · travel, housing, and meals covered · some Python
Research fellowships

Mentored, full-time research. Some research or strong engineering background helps; a PhD isn’t required.

  • MATSBerkeley or London · about 10 weeks, with an optional 6–12 month extension
  • LASR LabsLondon · about 13 weeks · stipend
  • Pivotal Research FellowshipLondon · about 9 weeks · stipend, housing, and compute · early career
  • ERA FellowshipCambridge, UK · summer · students and early-career researchers
  • Anthropic Fellows ProgramSan Francisco or London · about 4 months · stipend and compute · strong research or engineering portfolio
  • Astra FellowshipConstellation, Berkeley · about 6 months · fully funded
Policy fellowships

Research and government-facing roles. Open to all backgrounds.

  • GovAI Summer FellowshipLondon · 3 months · research or applied track
  • Horizon FellowshipWashington, DC · up to about 2 years · placement in government and think tanks
  • RAND Technology and Security Policy FellowsUS · about 1–2 years · strong analytical and writing background
  • TechCongressUS congressional offices · for technologists curious about policy
Field-building and generalist roles

For organizers, operators, and people who want to build things.

  • Pathfinder FellowshipFor students who organize AI safety groups at their university
  • Generator ResidencyBerkeley · 3 months · paid · for generalists building AI safety projects
Lab research and residencies

Paid roles inside AI labs, often for students or career switchers.

  • OpenAI ResidencySan Francisco · 6 months · paid, can convert to full-time
  • Google DeepMind Student Researcher12–24 weeks · for enrolled students
